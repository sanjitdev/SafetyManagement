# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Residents report public-space and infrastructure issues, then track their progress.
- Dispatchers review, prioritize, and assign reports to crews.
- Field crews complete assigned work and record status changes.
- City administrators configure services and oversee city operations.

## Product Purpose

CityFix gives residents one place to report city issues with photos and locations, and gives city teams a shared workflow to verify, route, assign, and track them through resolution.

## Positioning

CityFix connects a resident's report to the city team's operational workflow and returns visible progress to the resident.

## Operating Context

Residents use the web app to submit and follow reports. Dispatchers and administrators use role-based operational dashboards; field crews use the app while working on assigned issues.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing role permissions, routes, bilingual content, report workflow, map data, and operational actions while redesigning presentation.
- Do not fabricate operational metrics or notification counts; dashboard figures must come from existing data.
- The interface is a responsive web app.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is CityFix and the shared CityFix mark remains in use.
- The user supplied three dashboard images in `ui-design/` as visual references and requested their navigation and dashboard structure be carried through the product.
- Dark appearance is the default on every app page. Users may still select light appearance.

## Evidence on Hand

- The product problem statement and concept are in `docs/idea.md`.
- Reference images are `ui-design/admin-dashboard.png`, `ui-design/citien-dashboard.png`, and `ui-design/dispatcher-portal.png`.
- Live tickets, users, city configuration, and map locations come from the existing CityFix APIs.

## Product Principles

- Keep the report path clear for residents.
- Make work status and ownership easy for city teams to scan.
- Preserve accurate, live operational information.
- Keep language and interaction usable across resident, dispatcher, crew, and admin roles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Maintain keyboard focus visibility, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and the existing English/Bangla language support.
