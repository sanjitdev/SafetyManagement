4 role dashboards, rebuilt from the reference images in ui-design/
01 · Citizen Dashboard
The citizen's home. A hero band over a drawn city skyline carries the greeting; below it a blue "Report a Problem" CTA and three metric tiles, then the citizen's own recent reports beside a status-around-you map, and a six-month statistics line chart with the two civic bands. Every status carries a glyph, a word and a colour.

03 · Crew Dashboard
The field surface, and the only one of the four with no top bar — the greeting, the date chip and the bell share the content column so the chrome stays out of the job list.
