# Home / City Activity

**Route:** `/home`
**Scenario:** Rahim the Reporter — Reports a broken streetlight (screen 6 of 14)
**Purpose:** Show Rahim that his city is moving, make Report one tap away, and reward power-user behavior (10+ opens/day).

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** This page is now the re-skinned home mockup (`mockups/02-home-city-activity.html`). The full-bleed mobile layout (map + bottom sheet + floating Report CTA) is replaced with a **persistent map + side-panel activity feed + top-bar Report CTA** at large viewport (1440×900). At medium viewport (≤1024px) the side panel collapses below the map in a 50/50 split. The **floating Report FAB is gone**; the primary Report action lives in the top bar — a single button, always visible, on every viewport. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin rationale.

---

## User Context

Rahim lands here after Login (first run) or on every app reopen with a valid session. He is **wary-hoping** — first time, skeptical; on return visits, checking. He needs to see motion within 3 seconds or he assumes the system is dead. This is the default authenticated surface and the page he sees most. On a phone in the field, the page also has to be readable and tappable; on a laptop at home, the persistent map + side panel fits the larger surface.

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `home-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default, persists from earlier screens.
- **Layout (web-first, large viewport ≥ 1025px):** the page is split into a top bar (`--header-height-desktop`, 64px) plus a two-region content area below: **map on the left (1fr)** and **side panel on the right (`--report-side-panel`, 480px)**. The map fills the available height; the side panel sits flush against the right edge, scrolls independently.
- **Layout (medium viewport ≤ 1024px):** the same top bar collapses to `--header-height-mobile` (56px). The content area below reflows to a single column with a 50/50 vertical split — map on top (50% of remaining height), side panel below (50%). The side panel loses its right border and gains a top border.
- **At all viewports:** the **Report CTA lives in the top bar** (replaces the floating Report FAB). One button, one label, always visible.

### Top bar (always visible)

- **`home-topbar`** — height `--header-height-desktop` (64px) at large, `--header-height-mobile` (56px) at medium; `--color-surface-card` background; bottom border `--color-border`.
- **`home-brand`** (left) — small CityFix wordmark (dot + "CityFix" + middle dot + city name "ঢাকা উত্তর / Dhaka North"). Tapping the city name opens the city switcher.
- **`home-button-report`** (center) — **the primary Report CTA**. Solid primary fill, `--touch-large` (56px) tall, white text, label "নতুন রিপোর্ট / New report" with a small plus icon. Single tap → routes to `/report/location`. The button is **always visible** on Home and is the only way to start a new report from this page.
- **`home-my-reports`** (right) — text link "আমার রিপোর্ট / My reports".
- **`home-avatar`** (right, far) — circular avatar (initials), 32px. Tap → user menu (Profile, Settings, Language, Logout).

### Map (main view, left region at large / top region at medium)

- **`home-map`** — OpenStreetMap tiles via Leaflet or MapLibre (per WO-001 recommendation: MapLibre with OSM tiles). Fills the available map region at all viewports.
- Centered on city center by default; recenters on user's GPS once location permission granted (default zoom: city-wide).
- **Pin coloring by status** (consistent with mockup 02):
  - Open: `--color-map-pin-open` (red)
  - In progress: `--color-map-pin-progress` (amber)
  - Resolved: `--color-map-pin-resolved` (forest green)
- **Pin clustering:** at city zoom, multiple reports within ~30m collapse to a single cluster pin with count badge. Tap cluster → zoom to extent.
- **`home-pin-{ticket_id}`** — tappable; opens a small overlay card with category, ward, age, status, "View details" link → `/reports/{id}`.
- **Map controls (overlay, top-right of the map region):**
  - **`home-map-zoom-in`** / **`home-map-zoom-out`** — 36×36 white square buttons, stacked vertically.
- **Location pill (overlay, bottom-left of the map region):** a small white pill with a primary-color dot and a label of the current city area, e.g., "মিরপুর ১০ · আপনার এলাকা / Mirpur 10 · your area".

### Side panel (right region at large / bottom region at medium) — activity feed

- **`home-side-panel`** — `--color-surface-card` background, left border `--color-border` (large viewport only). Internally split into a sticky header and a scrollable body.
- **`home-side-panel-header`** — padding `--space-4` / `--space-5`, bottom border `--color-border`. Contains:
  - **`home-side-panel-title`** — H2 "শহরের কার্যকলাপ / City activity."
  - **`home-side-panel-subtitle`** — small caption "গত ২৪ ঘণ্টায় আপনার এলাকায় / In your area in the last 24 hours."
  - **`home-side-panel-filters`** — a row of filter pills: "All / সব" (default, primary fill), "New / নতুন", "In progress / চলমান", "Resolved / সমাধান". Tap → re-filters feed.
- **`home-side-panel-body`** — vertical scroll of activity cards. 12–16px padding. Independent scroll from the map.
- **`home-activity-card`** — each row: 56×56 thumbnail (gradient placeholder when no photo), category + short description, ward + age label, status pill on the right. 12px gap; cards separated by 8px (`--space-2`). Whole card is tappable → `/reports/{id}`.
- Pagination: 20 items per page; infinite scroll inside the side panel. "Load older" trigger at bottom.
- **New items at top** marked with a small "new" badge that fades after 5 seconds.

### First-authenticated open: notification permission prompt

- **`home-notification-prompt`** — shown once, immediately after first Home render, if notification permission is undetermined. Per UX Review revision #1, notifications are **deferred to Home after the first successful report** (not at splash).
- A non-blocking banner **inside the side panel** at the top of the activity body (large viewport) or at the top of the page (medium viewport): "আপনার রিপোর্টের আপডেট পেতে নোটিফিকেশন চালু করুন / Get notified when your reports update."
- Two buttons: "Enable / চালু করুন" (primary) and "Not now / এখন নয়" (text link).
- After user choice (either), the banner is hidden permanently (stored in `localStorage.notification_prompt_seen`).

### Persistent banner — notifications off

- **`home-banner-notifications-off`** — if notifications are denied AND `notification_prompt_seen === true`, show a small dismissible banner inside the side panel: "নোটিফিকেশন বন্ধ আছে — সেটিংস থেকে চালু করুন / Notifications are off — turn them on in Settings." Tappable Settings link; dismiss × on the right; dismissal persists per session.

### Empty / first-launch state

- If no reports exist (city just launched): map shows an empty city; side panel shows a centered message "শহরে কোনো রিপোর্ট নেই — প্রথমটি আইড করুন! / No reports yet — be the first to report!"
- The Report CTA in the top bar is highlighted with a brief one-time pulse on the first authenticated open to draw attention to it.

## Behavior

### Real-time updates

- The activity feed subscribes to the server via polling (10–30s) for new reports and status changes within the user's current map viewport. **WebSocket deferred to v1.1** (per WO-000).
- New items appear at the top of the feed with a subtle "new" highlight that fades after 5 seconds.
- Map pins animate as their status changes (open → in progress → resolved) with a 300ms color transition.

### Map–feed synchronization

- Filter pill selection applies to both map and feed simultaneously.
- Tapping a pin on the map highlights the corresponding activity card in the side panel (briefly). The card scrolls into view if needed.

### Permission handling

- **Location denied:** map centers on Dhaka/Chattogram city center; no recenter attempt. Side panel still functions.
- **Notifications denied:** no prompt re-asks; the persistent banner handles the long-term reminder.

### Network states

- **Online:** standard behavior.
- **Offline:** cached feed and map tiles shown; a slim banner at the top of the side panel says "Offline — showing recent activity / অফলাইন — সাম্প্রতিক দেখাচ্ছে". New pins / updates suppressed; report submission goes into a local queue (see Report Flow spec).
- **Slow network:** map tiles load low-res first; feed thumbnails show as colored placeholders until images arrive.

### Power-user behavior

- App reopens 10+ times/day: each open is a fresh Home render with up-to-date data. The "new" highlight on fresh items keeps the experience alive. The Report CTA in the top bar is the same on every viewport, so the path from "I see something broken" → "I am filing" is one consistent tap.

### Responsive behavior summary

- **Large viewport (≥1025px, e.g., desktop 1440×900):** map on the left, side panel on the right, Report CTA in the top bar.
- **Medium viewport (≤1024px, e.g., tablet 1024×768):** map on top, side panel below, Report CTA in the top bar (same button, same place).
- **Phone widths (≤480px):** the same two-region layout collapses further. The map region shrinks but stays usable (touch panning / pinch-zoom). The side panel drops to ~50% of the height. The Report CTA stays in the top bar. This is Rashid-class responsiveness for Rahim's pages — same web layout, smaller dimensions, no FAB and no bottom sheet.
- **At all viewports:** there is no FAB, no bottom sheet, no sticky-bottom Report button. The Report CTA is always in the top bar.

## States

### Default (online, no permission prompts active)

Top bar + map (left) + side panel (right) at large viewport; top bar + map (top) + side panel (bottom) at medium. Permission prompt hidden (already seen).

### First-authenticated open (notification permission undetermined)

Same as default, with `home-notification-prompt` visible at the top of the side-panel body.

### Notifications off (after dismissal)

Same as default, with `home-banner-notifications-off` visible until dismissed.

### Empty (no reports in city)

Map shows empty; side panel shows centered empty-state message. The Report CTA in the top bar pulses briefly on the first visit.

### Loading

Top bar + skeleton map (grey tiles) + skeleton feed items (grey rectangles, no text). Report CTA enabled.

### Offline

Top bar + cached map + cached feed + offline banner inside the side panel.

### Submitting (after Rahim taps Report CTA in the top bar)

Report CTA shows a brief spinner; transitions to `/report/location`.

## Success

Rahim lands on Home, sees 3–5 fresh activity items in the side panel, sees pins on the map around his location, taps the **নতুন রিপোর্ট** button in the top bar in under 10 seconds to start his broken-streetlight report. Same flow on phone widths — the button is in the same place, and the map + side panel still both render.

**Emotional outcome:** Within 3 seconds, Rahim feels the system is **alive**. Within 30 seconds, he has acted. On every subsequent open, he sees motion and trusts the platform.

## Open Questions

- **Map tile provider** — OpenStreetMap via Leaflet/MapLibre (default) vs. Google Maps. OSM is free and reliable in Bangladesh; Google requires API key + billing. **Default for v1: OSM.**
- **Activity feed pagination trigger** — "Load older" button at bottom vs. infinite scroll. **Default: infinite scroll** until feed performance is measured.
- **WebSocket vs. polling for real-time updates** — WebSocket is real-time but adds server complexity. Polling every 15s is good enough for v1. **Default: polling, with WebSocket as v1.1.**
- **Side drawer contents (Profile, Settings, Logout, About, Language)** — referenced from `home-avatar`. Each is a separate page; out of scope for this spec. **Default: spec'd in v2 sweep.**
- **Activity feed item display limit** — current spec says 20 per page with pagination. Should v1 support deeper history? **Default: 20.**
- **Search behavior** — placeholder removed in web re-skin. Full Search spec deferred to v2.
