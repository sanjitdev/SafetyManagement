# Report Flow — Photo Capture

**Route:** `/report/photo`
**Scenario:** Rahim the Reporter — Reports a broken streetlight (screen 8 of 14)
**Purpose:** Capture a photo of the problem so dispatchers and field crews know what they're dealing with.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** Photo Capture keeps its full-bleed camera viewfinder pattern — the camera is full-screen at every viewport because the camera *should* fill the screen on every device. The post-capture preview + Retake / Use controls now live inside a centered overlay card (max-width 480px) instead of a sticky-bottom pair. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin.

---

## User Context

Rahim arrives here from Location Confirm with his GPS locked in. He is looking at the broken streetlight. The screen opens his camera immediately so he can capture the photo with one motion.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `report-photo-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Full-bleed camera view with overlay controls.

### Header (overlay, transparent)

- **`report-photo-header`** — height 56px, transparent background, white text.
- **Left:** close ×. Tap → confirm dialog "রিপোর্ট বাতিল করবেন?" / "Discard report?".
- **Center:** "ধাপ ২ / ৪: ছবি" / "Step 2 of 4: Photo".
- **Right:** small gallery icon. Tap → opens device photo picker (alternative to camera).

### Progress indicator

- Same as Location Confirm: 2 of 4 segments filled.

### Camera view

- **`report-photo-camera-view`** — full-screen camera preview, rear-facing by default.
- **Permission:** if camera permission is undetermined, the system prompt fires here. If denied, fall back to photo picker only.
- A subtle framing hint at the top: "সমস্যার ছবি তুলুন" / "Photograph the problem".

### Capture controls (overlay, bottom)

- **`report-photo-button-capture`** — large round shutter, 72px diameter, white with primary-color border. Tap = take photo.
- Below the shutter, a small "ছবি বাছাই করুন" / "Pick from gallery" text link for users who'd rather upload an existing photo.
- During capture (shutter pressed): brief white flash, 100ms.

### Preview (after capture)

- After shutter fires, the captured image is shown full-screen for 3 seconds with an **overlay card centered on screen** (max-width 480px, `--color-surface-card`, padded) carrying two actions:
  - **`report-photo-button-retake`** — left of the card: "আবার তুলুন / Retake" (text button).
  - **`report-photo-button-use`** — right of the card: "এই ছবি ব্যবহার করুন / Use this photo" (primary button).
- After 3 seconds of no input, the preview auto-advances to **Use this photo** (default selection).
- Tap "Retake" → reopens camera.
- Tap "Use this photo" → routes to `/report/category`.

## Behavior

### Capture flow

1. Screen mounts → camera permission requested (if needed).
2. Camera opens, rear-facing.
3. Tap shutter → photo captured, preview shown.
4. Tap "Use this photo" or wait 3s → photo saved to local state, navigate to Category Pick.

### Gallery fallback

- If camera permission denied OR user taps gallery icon OR user taps "Pick from gallery" text link → opens device photo picker.
- Pick a photo → preview shown same as captured.
- Same Retake/Use flow.

### Compression

- Photos are client-side compressed to ~85% quality JPEG, max 1.5 MB before upload.
- EXIF metadata (especially GPS) is stripped on the client to avoid leaking precise home address beyond the pin already captured.

### Offline behavior

- Photo capture works offline; saved to local queue.
- Compression runs locally.
- Upload happens on next `/report/submit` call, deferred until reconnect.

### Back navigation

- × button → confirm dialog "Discard report?" with Discard / Keep editing.

## States

### Default (camera ready)

Full-screen camera preview; shutter visible; gallery icon visible.

### Permission denied

Camera area is replaced with a centered message: "ক্যামেরা ব্যবহারের অনুমতি নেই" / "Camera permission denied." A single button: "গ্যালারি থেকে বাছাই করুন" / "Pick from gallery."

### Capturing (shutter pressed)

Brief white flash overlay.

### Preview (after capture)

Full-screen preview of the captured photo with Retake / Use this photo overlays.

### Compressing (after Use tap)

Brief spinner overlay as the photo is compressed and saved to local state.

## Success

Rahim sees the broken streetlight through the camera, taps the shutter, sees his photo, taps "Use this photo" (or waits 3 seconds), and lands on Category Pick. Total time: under 5 seconds.

**Emotional outcome:** Rahim feels the platform is **capturing what he sees** — the actual broken thing — without making him describe it in words. The photo is what closes the loop later when Rashid arrives to fix it.

---

## Open Questions

- **Multiple photos per report** — should Rahim be able to attach more than one? **Default for v1: one photo.** Multiple-photo support is a v2 candidate; the design supports it (storage and state are photo-list-aware).
- **Required vs. optional photo** — currently optional via "Skip — describe instead." **Default: optional.** Skipping routes to Category Pick with no photo; the report is still submittable.
