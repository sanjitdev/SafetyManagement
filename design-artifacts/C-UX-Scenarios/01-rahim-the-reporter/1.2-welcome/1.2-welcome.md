# Welcome (first run only)

**Route:** `/welcome`
**Scenario:** Rahim the Reporter — Reports a broken streetlight (screen 2 of 14)
**Purpose:** Move Rahim to Register or Login in under three seconds. This screen is a fork, not a destination.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** This page is now the re-skinned hero mockup (`mockups/01-welcome.html`). Two-column web hero at large viewport (brand + headline left, abstract city map right), single column at medium. CTA pair (Register / Login) + trust markers. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin rationale.

---

## User Context

Rahim arrives here immediately after the splash screen, on his first open of the CityFix app. He has just installed the app from the app store after seeing it on Facebook. He has been on the app for about 3 seconds. His arrival state is **slightly impatient, mildly curious**. He wants to know what this is in one glance — not read a pitch.

This screen is shown **only on first run**. On any subsequent app open, Rahim is routed directly to Home (if authenticated) or Login (if not).

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `welcome-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default. Language toggle lives in the top-right corner of the left hero column at large viewport, and remains in the same position when the layout collapses to a single column at medium.
- **Layout (web-first, two-column at large viewport ≥ 1025px):** a 1.1fr / 1fr split. Left column carries brand wordmark at top, headline + lede in the middle, primary CTAs + trust strip at bottom. Right column carries the abstract city illustration (a stylized pin map; see `mockups/01-welcome.html`). The page is constrained to `--layout-desktop` (1440px) and centered.
- **Layout (single column at ≤ 1024px):** the right column is hidden, the left column reflows to full width with reduced vertical padding. Same CTAs, same headline, same trust strip — only the illustration drops.
- **Background:** `--color-surface-page`. No full-bleed color wash.

### Header

- **`welcome-header`** — there is no longer a 56px sticky header at this page; the brand wordmark sits at the top of the left column and acts as the visual anchor. **No back arrow** (nothing to go back to on first run).
- **Right side (top of left column at large; same position at medium):** `welcome-button-language-toggle` — small text button labeled "EN" / "বাংলা". Tap toggles the whole screen's language and persists the choice to local storage.

### Hero block

Vertically centered within the left column, this block carries the entire message.

- **`welcome-headline`** — H1, two-line break on Bangla.
  - **Bangla:** "আপনার শহরের<br/>আওয়াজ।"
  - **English:** "Voice of your city."

- **`welcome-subhead`** (`welcome-lede`) — supporting paragraph, two lines on Bangla.
  - **Bangla:** "ভাঙা রাস্তার আলো, ড্রেনে আটকে থাকা পানি, জমে থাকা আবর্জনা — রিপোর্ট করুন, অগ্রগতি দেখুন, সমাধানের প্রমাণ পান।"
  - **English:** "Report broken streetlights, blocked drains, and piled garbage — track the fix, see proof of resolution."

No additional paragraphs. No marketing copy.

### Right column (illustration, large viewport only)

- **`welcome-illustration`** — at viewport widths ≥ 1025px the right column carries an abstract city illustration: a 520×520 pin map with three status-pin clusters (open / in-progress / resolved) over stylized roads, park, water. Decorative only — no interaction. The illustration hides at medium viewport (≤ 1024px).

### Primary actions (CTA pair)

Two equal-width buttons rendered in a 1fr / 1fr grid, max-width 520px, stacked at the bottom of the left column. This is **not** a sticky-bottom CTA — the buttons live inside the hero column at all viewports. (Sticky-bottom CTAs are reserved for report-flow pages where Rahim is mid-task and needs the action to follow his thumb.)

- **`welcome-button-register`** — solid primary fill, `--touch-cta` (64px) tall, white text.
  - **Label (BN):** "নিবন্ধন করুন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Register"
  - Tap → routes to `/register`.

- **`welcome-button-login`** — secondary outline style, `--touch-cta` (64px) tall, ink text, `--color-border-strong` border.
  - **Label (BN):** "লগইন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Log in"
  - Tap → routes to `/login`.

### Trust strip

Below the CTA pair, a single horizontal row of three trust markers separated by light dividers:

- **`welcome-trust-government`** — "সরকার পরিচালিত / Government operated" (shield icon)
- **`welcome-trust-secure`** — "তথ্য সুরক্ষিত / Your data is protected" (lock icon)
- **`welcome-trust-language`** — "বাংলা · English" (globe icon)

All three items use `--text-caption` and `--color-ink-tertiary`. Markers wrap to two lines if viewport is narrower than ~480px.

## Behavior

### First-run gate

- On app open, before any other logic runs, the app checks `localStorage.welcome_seen`.
- **`welcome_seen === true`** → skip this screen entirely and route directly to:
  - `/home` if an auth token is present and not expired
  - `/login` otherwise
- **`welcome_seen !== true`** (first run, or local storage cleared) → render this screen.
- When Rahim reaches this screen, set `welcome_seen = true` **as soon as the screen renders** (not after he taps Register). This means an interrupted first open (he taps home, comes back) still skips Welcome on the next launch.

### Permission prompts

**No permission prompts on this screen.** Location, notifications, and camera permissions are deferred to:
- Location: on the first Report Flow (Location Confirm screen) — the moment it's relevant.
- Notifications: on Home (after auth), with a single combined prompt.
- Camera: on the first time the user taps Photo Capture.

This avoids an *ask-fatigue* moment on Welcome, where every request for permission narrows Rahim's willingness to engage.

### Language toggle

- Tap `welcome-button-language-toggle` → toggle every visible string on the screen. No page reload.
- The choice persists in `localStorage.preferred_language` and is read on every subsequent screen (Registration, OTP, Home, etc.).
- Available values: `"bn"` (default) and `"en"`.

## States

### Default (first run, Bangla)

Two-column hero (large viewport) or single-column hero (medium viewport). Headline + lede in the left column. CTA pair + trust strip at the bottom of the left column. Right column carries the illustration at large viewport only. Language toggle shows "EN".

### Default (first run, English)

Same layout, all copy in English. Language toggle shows "বাংলা".

### Returning user (skip)

This screen never renders for users with `welcome_seen === true`. Returning authenticated users land on Home; returning unauthenticated users land on Login. The screen is **only** rendered for the first-run path.

## Success

Rahim arrives on Welcome, sees two clear options, taps **"রিপোর্ট করতে নিবন্ধন করুন"** within 5 seconds. The app routes to `/register`. From the splash to the registration form is roughly 8 seconds of clean, focused flow with zero marketing, zero permissions, zero forms in his way.

**Emotional outcome:** Rahim feels **oriented, not pitched at**. He knows what the app does in two sentences and what his next tap will do. The screen earns him past the part of the funnel where most civic apps lose new users.

## Open Questions

- **Hero block visual treatment** — currently text-only. A future v2 might add a single muted illustration (a city skyline, a hand holding a phone) below the subhead. Decision deferred — text-first is the safer v1 call.
- **Background color / treatment** — solid neutral vs. soft gradient vs. brand color wash. Final call belongs in token extraction, not here.
- **Bangla headline tone** — "আপনার শহর ঠিক করুন" reads confident and direct. If pre-launch user testing shows it lands as too brash for civic decorum, alternative: "শহরকে ভালো রাখুন।" — slightly softer. **Default: confident tone.**
