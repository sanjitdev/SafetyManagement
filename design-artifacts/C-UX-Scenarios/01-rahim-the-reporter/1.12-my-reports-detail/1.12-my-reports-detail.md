# My Reports Detail

**Route:** `/reports/:ticket_id`
**Scenario:** Rahim the Reporter — Reports a broken streetlight (screen 12 of 14)
**Purpose:** Show Rahim everything about his own report — full timeline, before/after photos, and the department handling it.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** This page is now the re-skinned detail mockup (`mockups/04-my-reports-detail.html`). The mobile single-column scroll is replaced with a **top bar + two-column content area** at large viewport (1440×900): left column (1.5fr) carries hero, before/after photo pair, and status trail; right column (1fr) carries metadata card and feedback prompt. At medium viewport (≤1024px) the right column stacks below the left. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin rationale.

---

## User Context

Rahim arrives here from the Submission Confirmation, from the activity feed, or from a push notification. He wants to see what happened with his report — and to confirm that someone is on it. On a phone in the field, the page also has to be readable and tappable; on a laptop at home, the two-column layout fits the larger surface.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `my-report-detail-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout (web-first, large viewport ≥ 1025px):** the page is split into a top bar (`--header-height-desktop`, 64px) + a two-column content area below, constrained to `--layout-desktop` (1440px) and centered, with `--space-6` / `--space-5` padding. Left column is **1.5fr** and carries hero, before/after photo pair, and status trail. Right column is **1fr** and carries the metadata card and (after resolution) the feedback prompt.
- **Layout (medium viewport ≤ 1024px):** the same top bar collapses to `--header-height-mobile` (56px). The content area reflows to a single column with `--space-5` / `--space-4` padding. Hero, photo pair, and trail render in order; metadata card stacks below; feedback prompt stacks below metadata.
- **At all viewports:** there is **no sticky-bottom action**. The feedback prompt is in-flow at the bottom of the right column (or below the metadata at medium).

### Top bar

- **`my-report-detail-topbar`** — same surface and height tokens as the rest of the app's top bars.
- **`my-report-detail-brand`** (left) — CityFix wordmark + breadcrumb "আমার রিপোর্টসমূহ / My reports".
- **`my-report-detail-link-my-reports`** (right) — text link "আমার রিপোর্ট / My reports".
- **`my-report-detail-link-home`** (right) — text link "হোম / Home".
- **`my-report-detail-avatar`** (right, far) — circular avatar (initials), 32px. Tap → user menu.

### Hero (top of left column at large / top of content at medium)

- **`my-report-detail-status-pill`** — small status pill (resolved / routed / in-progress / submitted) with color from `--color-status-*` tokens. Top-left.
- **`my-report-detail-ticket-id`** — small monospace text in `--color-ink-tertiary` next to the status pill. Example: "#TIX-1234".
- **`my-report-detail-title`** — H1, "রাস্তার আলো নেই · পোল #14B / Streetlight out · Pole #14B".
- **`my-report-detail-subtitle`** — small caption in `--color-ink-secondary`, e.g., "ওয়ার্ড ১৪, মিরপুর · ৪ দিন আগে জমা / Ward 14, Mirpur · submitted 4 days ago".

### Before / after photo pair (left column, below hero)

- **`my-report-detail-photo-pair`** — two-column grid (1fr / 1fr) at all viewports. Each photo block is `aspect-ratio: 4 / 3`, `--radius-md` corners, `--color-border` border, with a small "আগে / Before" or "পরে / After" label badge in the top-left corner of each block.
- **Single after-photo** (most cases): the right block shows the single after-photo. A small horizontal-scroll hint at the right edge if there are 2–3 after-photos (per UX Review revision #6 — multi-photo default cap = 3, narrow-category cap = 2, mandatory minimum = 1).
- **Multiple after-photos (2–3):** the right block becomes a horizontal scroll of all after-photos; the last visible block shows a "+n" overlay indicator. Same Area Label `my-report-detail-photo-pair`.
- **No after-photo yet** (status: submitted / routed / in-progress): the right block is replaced by a single "before" photo that takes the full width; a small caption "আগে ছবি · সমাধানের পরে আপডেট হবে / Before photo · after-photo will appear on resolution" sits below.

### Status trail (left column, below photo pair)

- **`my-report-detail-status-trail`** — same vertical-timeline pattern as the Submission Confirmation page, but always showing all 4 steps with the current state. Trail card has a small "অগ্রগতি / Progress" caption header, `--text-micro` `--color-ink-tertiary` letter-spaced. 4 steps: জমা হয়েছে / রাউটেড / মাঠ ক্রু কাজ শুরু করেছে / সমাধান নিশ্চিত হয়েছে. Each step's time + actor shown in `--text-caption` `--color-ink-tertiary`.
- **Internal actors are anonymized for citizens** — Rahim sees "ডিসপ্যাচার হাবিব" is **not** shown; he sees "সিটি কর্পোরেশন / City corporation" or "ক্রু এ / Crew A" only. This is non-negotiable per WO-001 Do-Not-Change #6.

### Right column (large viewport) / stacks below at medium

- **`my-report-detail-meta-card`** — `--color-surface-card` with `--color-border` border, `--radius-md` corners, `--space-5` padding. A 2-column grid (1fr / 1fr) inside the card with labeled rows:
  - **বিভাগ / Category** — e.g., "বৈদ্যুতিক / Electrical"
  - **ওয়ার্ড / Ward** — e.g., "১৪, মিরপুর / 14, Mirpur"
  - **SLA** — e.g., "৪৮ ঘণ্টা / 48 hours"
  - **সমাধানের সময় / Time to resolve** — e.g., "৩.২ দিন / 3.2 days" (only after resolution)
- Each label uses `--text-micro` `--color-ink-tertiary` letter-spaced; each value uses `--text-body-sm` `--weight-medium`.

### Feedback prompt (right column, after resolution only)

- **`my-report-detail-feedback`** — only shown when status is Resolved. `--color-primary-soft` background, `--color-primary` left border (3px), `--radius-sm` corners, `--space-4` padding. Matches `mockups/04-my-reports-detail.html` exactly.
- **Heading:** "সমাধান কি সন্তোষজনক? / Was the fix satisfactory?"
- **Helper:** "আপনার মতামত আমাদের ডিসপ্যাচারদের কাজের মান উন্নত করে / Your feedback helps us improve dispatcher quality."
- Two buttons in a row:
  - **`my-report-detail-feedback-positive`** — outline primary, label "হ্যাঁ, সন্তুষ্ট / Yes, satisfied".
  - **`my-report-detail-feedback-negative`** — outline neutral, label "আরও বিস্তারিত / More details".
- Tapping either submits feedback and collapses the prompt to a "ধন্যবাদ প্রতিক্রিয়ার জন্য / Thanks for your feedback" acknowledgment.

### Note (if Rahim added one)

- **`my-report-detail-note`** — quoted block at the bottom of the left column. Italic text in `--color-ink-secondary`, with a left border. Renders Rahim's typed note from the report flow. If empty: section is hidden.

### "Why hasn't this been fixed yet?" link

- **`my-report-detail-link-why-delayed`** — small text link at the bottom of the right column, only visible after 7+ days in "Routed" or "In progress" state without resolution.
- **Label (BN):** "কেন এখনো সমাধান হয়নি?"
- **Label (EN):** "Why hasn't this been fixed yet?"
- Tap → opens a small modal with an honest explanation: "এই রিপোর্টটি বরাদ্দ আছে। সমাধানের সময় রিপোর্টের ধরন অনুযায়ী আলাদা হয়। / This report is assigned. Resolution time varies by report type." With a "Contact support / সাপোর্টে যোগাযোগ" button (opens `mailto:support@cityfix.bd`).

### Share button (top bar)

- **`my-report-detail-button-share`** — small icon button in the top bar. Generates a deep link to this report and copies it to clipboard. Useful for sharing proof of a fix with neighbors or on social media.

## Behavior

### Real-time updates

- Status trail polls every 10–30s (per WO-000 platform foundation) while the screen is foregrounded.
- New photos ("after" photo arrives when Rashid marks resolved) animate in.

### Photo zoom

- Tap a photo → opens in full-screen lightbox with pinch-to-zoom.

### Feedback submission

- POSTs `{ticket_id, feedback: "positive" | "negative"}` to `/api/v1/reports/{ticket_id}/feedback`.
- One feedback per ticket — once submitted, the prompt collapses permanently to the acknowledgment.

### Sharing

- Tap the share button in the top bar → generates a deep link, copies to clipboard, shows a small toast "লিঙ্ক কপি হয়েছে / Link copied."

## States

### Submitted / Routed (no after-photo)

Hero shows "before" photo at full width; status trail with 1 or 2 steps complete; metadata card visible; feedback prompt hidden (only shows on resolve); "Why hasn't this been fixed yet?" link hidden (only after 7+ days).

### In progress (no after-photo)

Same as above; status trail with 3 steps complete.

### Resolved (with after-photo)

Hero shows before/after pair; all 4 steps complete; feedback prompt visible; "Why hasn't this been fixed yet?" link hidden (resolved).

### Aged unfixed (7+ days)

Same as the in-progress state, plus the "Why hasn't this been fixed yet?" link visible at the bottom of the right column.

### Feedback already submitted

Feedback prompt replaced with "ধন্যবাদ প্রতিক্রিয়ার জন্য / Thanks for your feedback" message (still inside the primary-soft card).

### Offline

Body shows cached content with a slim offline banner at the top of the page (inside the top bar's bottom border zone). Status polling paused; banner says "অফলাইন — সর্বশেষ ডেটা দেখাচ্ছে / Offline — showing last cached data."

## Success

Rahim sees his report, sees motion (status updates), sees the before/after when resolved, and submits feedback. He feels **the loop has closed**. He will return to this screen on future reports. The page reads identically on his phone (stacked) and his laptop (two-column).

**Emotional outcome:** This is where Rahim's *trust* is either confirmed or eroded. If the status trail is accurate and the resolution is honest, his next report is unhesitating. If anything feels broken (stuck status, missing after-photo, unacknowledged feedback), he may not return.

## Open Questions

- **Feedback granularity** — currently binary (positive/negative). **Default for v1: binary.** Star-rating or comment-on-resolve deferred to v2.
- **Map snippet** — should this page include a small map showing where the report is? **Default for v1: no.** Location is shown as text (ward name). Adding a map snippet is a v2 enhancement.
