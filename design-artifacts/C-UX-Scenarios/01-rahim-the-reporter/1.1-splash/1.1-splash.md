# App Open / Splash

**Route:** `/` (root) → routes to `/welcome`, `/login`, or `/home` based on auth state
**Scenario:** Rahim the Reporter — Reports a broken streetlight (screen 1 of 14)
**Purpose:** Open the app, request the permissions Rahim needs to act, and route him to the right place in under 2 seconds.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** CityFix is web-first. Splash content is centered on a neutral surface and renders inside `--layout-mobile-max` (480px) on small screens, full-screen on desktop. Same permissions, same routes, same render budget. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin.

---

## User Context

Rahim taps the CityFix icon on his phone. He expects a brief, calm, professional experience — not a marketing splash. The page must show the app is alive, request the permissions Rahim needs to actually use the platform (location, notifications), and route him onward.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `splash-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Web-first. Centered splash visual on a calm neutral surface (`--color-surface-page`). Same content at every viewport; the splash never reflows into a sidebar. On phone widths the splash is full-screen; on desktop it sits inside a 480px column. The `--header-height-desktop` token and full background are unused here — splash owns the whole frame.
- **Render time:** ≤ 2 seconds on a mid-range Android device / a mid-range laptop on cable broadband.

### Splash visual

- **`splash-logo`** — the CityFix wordmark, centered.
- Below the wordmark: a small caption "আপনার শহরের আওয়াজ / Voice of your city."
- A subtle progress indicator (small dot or thin spinner) below the caption — signals the app is loading.

### Permission preflight (after render)

Once the splash finishes rendering, the app checks two permissions:

#### Location

- **`splash-permission-location`** — native OS dialog.
- **Rationale (BN):** "আপনার কাছাকাছি রিপোর্ট দেখাতে অবস্থান প্রয়োজন / We use your location to show reports near you."
- **Rationale (EN):** "We use your location to show reports near you."
- "While using the app" is the requested scope.
- **Asked at splash:** Yes. Location powers the Home map regardless of intent to report — Rahim sees immediate value (pins on the map near him) which lowers drop-off risk.

#### Notifications

- **`splash-permission-notifications`** — native OS dialog.
- **Rationale (BN):** "আপনার রিপোর্ট সমাধান হলে জানানো হবে / We'll notify you when your report is fixed."
- **Rationale (EN):** "We'll notify you when your report is fixed."
- **Asked at splash:** No — deferred to Home page after first successful report (see Home spec). Rahim is more likely to grant notifications after he's seen the platform act on his behalf.

### Routing logic

After both permission prompts have resolved (or been dismissed), the app checks auth state:

- **No JWT in storage:** route to `/welcome` (first-run only) → `/register`.
- **JWT in storage but stale (>30 days):** route to `/login`.
- **Valid JWT:** route to `/home`.

If permission prompts are dismissed (denied), the app still routes onward — the platform degrades gracefully.

## Behavior

### Permission order

- **Location is requested at splash** (single prompt). If granted, Home can show nearby pins immediately.
- **Notifications are requested on the Home page after the user's first successful report** (see Home spec). If location is denied at splash, the Home page degrades gracefully (no nearby pins; user can still browse) and the notification prompt is unaffected.
- Both prompts are non-blocking — Rahim can dismiss either and still proceed.

### Splash persistence

- The splash visual stays visible until the JS bundle finishes loading and the first route is determined.
- On slow networks, the splash may remain for up to 4 seconds before the app transitions. (A loading caption appears if it takes longer than 2 seconds: "লোড হচ্ছে… / Loading…")

### Cold-start vs. warm-start

- **Cold start (app closed):** splash is shown; full permission flow runs.
- **Warm start (app backgrounded):** splash is skipped; the user returns to the last foregrounded route. Permission prompts do **not** re-fire.

### Skip on subsequent launches

- After the first successful permission flow, subsequent launches do not re-prompt — unless the user explicitly resets permissions in their device settings.
- Notification permission may still be requested once on the Home page (after first report), independently of location.

### Failure to load

- If the JS bundle fails to load (network error), the splash stays visible with a "Retry" button and an error caption: "সংযোগ ব্যর্থ — আবার চেষ্টা করুন / Connection failed — try again."

## States

### Default (cold start, first run)

Splash visible; permission prompts pending.

### Permission prompt visible

Native OS dialog on top of the splash.

### Auth check in progress

Splash visible; spinner animating.

### Routing (transitioning)

Splash fades out (200ms); the destination page fades in.

### Already signed in

Splash is brief (≤ 1 second); routes directly to `/home`.

### Bundle load failure

Error caption + Retry button.

### Backgrounded (warm start)

Splash skipped entirely.

## Success

Rahim taps the CityFix icon. Splash shows for 1.5 seconds. Location prompt appears; he taps Allow. Notification prompt appears; he taps Allow. App routes to `/welcome` (because he has no account yet). Total time: 4 seconds.

**Emotional outcome:** Rahim feels the platform is **fast and respectful**. No marketing detour, no forced tutorial. He grants the permissions because the rationale is honest, and he's on his way.

## Open Questions

- **Welcome screen vs. permissions** — **v1 default (revised after UX review):** location is requested at splash (because it powers Home's nearby-pins map and gives Rahim immediate value), but **notifications are deferred** to the Home page after the user's first successful report (so the rationale lands after the platform has acted on Rahim's behalf once).
- **Splash + Home permission interplay** — if location is denied at splash, Home's nearby-pins degrade silently; if notifications are denied on Home, the resolution notification path is skipped (the resolution is still visible in My Reports Detail).
