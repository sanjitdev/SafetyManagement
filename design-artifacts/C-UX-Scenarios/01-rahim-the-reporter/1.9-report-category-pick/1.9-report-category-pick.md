# Report Flow — Category Pick

**Route:** `/report/category`
**Scenario:** Rahim the Reporter — Reports a broken streetlight (screen 9 of 14)
**Purpose:** Let Rahim pick what kind of problem he's reporting so the report routes to the right department.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** Category Pick keeps its grid pattern but renders inside a centered card (max-width 640px) on a neutral surface at all viewports. At large viewport the grid is **3 columns × 2 rows**; at medium / phone widths it collapses to **2 columns × 3 rows**. The header (back arrow + step label) lives in the centered card, not as a sticky top bar. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin.

---

## User Context

Rahim arrives here from Photo Capture with location and photo locked in. He's still on the street, looking at the broken streetlight. He needs to tap one category and move on.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `report-category-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Web-first centered card. The card is constrained to `max-width: 640px` at all viewports, sitting on a calm neutral background (`--color-surface-page`). Inside the card: header (back arrow + step label) + progress bar + title block + grid of category tiles. No sticky-top header and no sticky-bottom CTA — everything lives inside the card.

### Header

- **`report-category-header`** — height 56px.
- **Left:** back arrow `<`. Routes to `/report/photo`.
- **Center:** "ধাপ ৩ / ৪: ক্যাটাগরি" / "Step 3 of 4: Category".

### Progress indicator

3 of 4 segments filled.

### Title block

- **`report-category-title`** — H1. "কী সমস্যা?" / "What's the problem?"
- **`report-category-subtitle`** — small helper text. "একটি বেছে নিন" / "Pick one."

### Category grid

- **`report-category-grid`** — responsive grid. **At large viewport (≥1025px):** 3 columns × 2 rows. **At medium / phone widths (≤1024px):** 2 columns × 3 rows. Each tile is `min-height: 120px`, rounded, with icon and Bangla + English label. 12px gap.
- Categories (6 tiles, in this order):
  1. **`report-category-tile-streetlight`** — icon (lightbulb), "রাস্তার আলো / Streetlight"
  2. **`report-category-tile-road`** — icon (pothole), "রাস্তা / Road"
  3. **`report-category-tile-garbage`** — icon (trash), "আবর্জনা / Garbage"
  4. **`report-category-tile-drain`** — icon (water), "নর্দমা / Drain"
  5. **`report-category-tile-sanitation`** — icon (stray dog / sanitation), "স্যানিটেশন / Sanitation"
  6. **`report-category-tile-other`** — icon (more / dots), "অন্যান্য / Other"
- Tap a tile → routes to `/report/note`.
- The **previously tapped** tile is briefly highlighted with a primary-color border (300ms transition) before navigation.

### Footer

- A small text link, centered below the grid: "আগের ধাপে ফিরে যান" / "Back to previous step". Same as the back arrow.

---

## Behavior

### Selection flow

1. Rahim taps a category tile.
2. Tile highlights briefly.
3. App navigates to `/report/note` with category preserved.

### Category → department mapping

- The mapping is **configured per city** by Aamir (the admin). For Dhaka (default): Streetlight → Electrical, Road → Roads, Garbage → Sanitation, Drain → Drainage/Waterlogging, Sanitation → Sanitation, Other → triage queue.
- The category tile only stores the category; the department is resolved server-side at submission time.

### Back navigation

- Back arrow → `/report/photo` (location and photo preserved).

---

## States

### Default

Six tiles in a 2-column grid; no selection yet.

### Tile tapped (transition)

Tile shows primary-color border for ~300ms before navigation.

### Loading (in case category list comes from API)

Skeleton tiles (grey rectangles) instead of icons and labels.

---

## Success

Rahim looks at the broken streetlight, taps the Streetlight tile, and lands on Note (the last step). Total time on this screen: under 2 seconds.

**Emotional outcome:** This is the lightest step in the flow — almost no friction. Rahim has effectively *categorized* the city wellness issue he just observed, and that's the platform's promise: that the system handles the routing, not him.

---

## Open Questions

- **Custom category labels per city** — for Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, etc., categories might want different names. **Default:** category labels are city-configurable (Aamir's admin tool sets them). Spec uses Dhaka defaults.
- **Sub-categories** — should "Road" break into "Pothole / Damaged surface / Missing sign"? **Default for v1: no.** Sub-categories are a v2 candidate.
