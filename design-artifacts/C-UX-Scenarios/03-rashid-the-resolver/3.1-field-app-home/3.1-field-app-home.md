# Field App Home (Rashid's start of day)

**Route:** `/field`
**Scenario:** Rashid the Resolver — Fixes his first job of the day (screen 1 of 5)
**Purpose:** Give Rashid a focused, glanceable view of his queue for the day, designed for one-handed phone use in the field.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** CityFix is web-first. Rashid's pages are also web-first, but **stay usable at phone widths** (per Feature #4 — field crew may be on a phone). Layout: top bar + centered content column (max-width 720px) + collapsible quick-stats panel + scrollable queue list. The bottom sticky "Start next job" CTA is replaced with an in-flow primary button at the bottom of the content column. The content column reflows cleanly down to 360px phone widths. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin.

---

## User Context

Rashid pulls up CityFix on his phone as he leaves home. He wants to see his jobs for the day, sorted by what to do first. He is one-handed, in bright sunlight, possibly on a moving motorcycle or rickshaw. The page must work at a glance.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `field-home-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Web-first centered content column on a calm neutral surface. Column max-width 720px. Inside: greeting strip, quick-stats panel (collapsible), today's queue list, and an in-flow "Start next job" CTA at the bottom of the column.
- **At phone widths (≤ 480px):** the column shrinks to 360px; touch targets stay ≥ 56px; the greeting strip wraps; the queue list rows remain 72×72 thumbnails. The layout is identical to larger viewports, just narrower.
- **Background:** white in light mode, near-black in dark mode. High contrast for sunlight. The page uses the default theme, not `.theme-outdoor` — that theme is reserved for the Status: In Progress page (where Rashid is hands-busy). Here, Rashid's eyes scan the queue; standard contrast is fine.

### Top bar (web-first)

- **`field-home-topbar`** — `--header-height-desktop` (64px) at large viewport, `--header-height-mobile` (56px) at medium and phone widths.
- **Left:** city selector (compact, "ঢাকা উত্তর / Dhaka North"). Tap → scope switcher.
- **Center:** "আজকের কাজ / Today's jobs".
- **Right:** avatar icon (initials, 32px circular). Tap → user menu (name, sign out).

### Greeting strip

- **`field-home-greeting`** — full-width strip, 80px tall.
- **Text (BN):** "সুপ্রভাত, রশিদ · আজ ৪টি কাজ / Good morning, Rashid · 4 jobs today."
- A small weather-like icon (sun/rain) reflecting current Dhaka conditions — a quiet human touch.
- **Refresh-location button** (`field-home-button-refresh-location`) — small icon at the right of the greeting strip.
  - **Label (BN):** "অবস্থান হালনাগাদ করুন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Refresh location"
  - One-shot: taps trigger a single GPS fix to update distance / ETA calculations on the queue rows. Does **not** run continuously (battery + privacy tradeoff in Dhaka's heat).
  - If GPS is unavailable: button shows a small warning icon and a tooltip "GPS অনুপলব্ধ / GPS unavailable."

### Today's queue list

- **`field-home-queue-list`** — vertical scroll.
- **`field-home-queue-row-{ticket_id}`** — each row:
  - Photo thumbnail (72×72, left)
  - Category icon + label (e.g., "রাস্তার আলো / Streetlight")
  - Address — first 30 chars
  - Distance from Rashid's last known location ("০.৪ কিমি / 0.4 km")
  - ETA suggestion ("৮ মিনিট / 8 min")
  - Status pill
- Sorted by: status (in-progress first, then new, then completed) → distance (near first).
- Pull-to-refresh at the top.

### Empty state

- If Rashid has no jobs today: "আজ কোনো কাজ নেই — বিশ্রাম নিন / No jobs today — rest up."

### Quick stats (collapsible)

- **`field-home-stats-collapsed`** — small collapsible row.
- "আজ: ২ সম্পন্ন / ১ চলমান / Today: 2 done / 1 in progress"
- Tap → expands to show "সপ্তাহে ১৪ সম্পন্ন / 14 this week."

### Primary action (in-flow, bottom of content column)

- **`field-home-button-start-first`** — primary CTA, in-flow at the bottom of the content column, full-width, `--touch-large` (56px) tall. **Not** sticky-bottom.
- Only visible when there's at least one in-progress or new job.
- **Label (BN):** "পরবর্তী কাজ শুরু করুন"
- **Label (EN):** "Start next job"
- Tap → opens the next ticket's assignment detail page (in priority order).

## Behavior

### PWA install prompt

- On second or later visit, if the app is not yet installed as a PWA: a small one-time banner appears at the top: "হোম স্ক্রিনে যোগ করুন — দ্রুত অ্যাক্সেস / Add to home screen — faster access." Dismiss persists.

### Real-time updates

- Polls every 15s (Rashid is in motion; battery is a concern).
- New assignments animate in at the top with a pulse.

### Distance calculation

- Computed using Rashid's last known location — either from a previous check-in, an assignment "Start work" tap, or a manual "Refresh location" tap on the greeting strip (one-shot, not continuous).
- Distance is approximate and labeled as such.
- One-shot refresh exists because continuous GPS in Dhaka's heat + older Android devices is a battery killer and crews will uninstall the app.

### Tapping a row

- Tap → routes to `/field/jobs/{ticket_id}` (assignment detail).

### Offline mode

- If the device is offline, the list shows the last cached state with a slim offline banner: "অফলাইনে আছেন — পুরোনো ডেটা দেখাচ্ছে / Offline — showing last cached data."

### Background sync

- When Rashid completes a job offline, the action is queued and synced on reconnect. He sees a small "সিঙ্ক হচ্ছে / Syncing…" badge on the affected row.

## States

### Default (online, jobs present)

Top bar + greeting strip + collapsible quick-stats + queue list + in-flow "Start next job" CTA. All inside the 720px content column. The column is centered on the page; the page background is `--color-surface-page`.

### Empty (no jobs)

Greeting + centered empty state + (collapsible) weekly stats. No "Start next job" CTA visible.

### Loading

Skeleton rows in the queue list inside the content column. Top bar visible; greeting strip visible with a skeleton shimmer; CTA hidden.

### Offline

Last cached state visible with offline banner at the top of the content column (just below the top bar).

### New job arrived (mid-session)

New row animates in at the top of the queue list with a pulse; badge increment on header.

### PWA install prompt

One-time dismissible banner at the top of the content column (not a sticky bottom — it's an in-flow dismissible).

---

## Success

Rashid opens the app, sees 4 jobs in his queue, taps "Start next job" at the bottom of the column, and is on his way within 15 seconds. The flow reads identically on his phone (column is 360px wide, CTA at the bottom) and on a teammate's laptop at the depot (column is 720px wide, CTA at the bottom of the same column).

**Emotional outcome:** Rashid feels **equipped** — he knows what's coming, where, and how far. No surprises, no fumbling through emails or phone calls.
