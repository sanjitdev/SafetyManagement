# Resolve — Before / After Capture

**Route:** `/field/jobs/{ticket_id}/resolve`
**Scenario:** Rashid the Resolver — Fixes his first job of the day (screen 4 of 5)
**Purpose:** Guide Rashid to capture a clear "after" photo and submit the resolution, with minimum friction.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** Resolve Capture is a **full-bleed page** at every viewport (same rationale as Status: In Progress — Rashid is hands-busy and owns the whole screen). The camera viewfinder is full-screen at all viewports; the post-capture form (note, parts, submit) renders inside an **overlay sheet** that slides up from the bottom of the viewport — like a bottom-sheet pattern but constrained to the content column at large viewports. The Submit button is in-flow inside the overlay, not sticky. See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin.

---

## User Context

Rashid has finished the work. He needs to take a single, clear photo of the fixed thing and submit. The "before / after" pair is the proof — the citizen sees it, the dispatcher confirms it, and the loop closes. Rashid should not have to think.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `field-job-resolve-capture-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Web-first **full-bleed** during camera capture (the viewfinder owns the entire viewport at every breakpoint). After at least one capture, the form section renders inside an **overlay sheet** that slides up from the bottom of the viewport:
  - **At phone widths (≤ 480px):** the overlay sheet occupies the bottom ~50% of the viewport, sits over the camera viewfinder, drag-handle at the top.
  - **At tablet/desktop widths:** the overlay sheet is constrained to the content column (max-width 720px), centered horizontally at the bottom of the viewport with `--space-5` bottom margin; same drag-handle, same content.
- **Background:** dark in camera mode for better viewfinder contrast. The overlay sheet uses `--color-surface-card` with `--shadow-modal`.

### Header (full-bleed overlay)

- **`field-job-resolve-header`** — height 64px overlay on top of the camera viewfinder, transparent until backdrop close.
- **Left:** back arrow `<`. Routes back to the in-progress page.
- **Center:** "সমাধানের প্রমাণ / Proof of fix."
- **Right:** help icon (?). Tap → shows a one-screen overlay with example photos.

### Before thumbnail

- **`field-job-resolve-before-thumb`** — small, 64×64, top-right of the header. Tap → opens lightbox showing the "before" photo for reference.

### Camera block

- **`field-job-resolve-camera-block`** — full-width viewfinder, ~360px tall.
- Centered reticle to help frame the shot.
- Bottom-right shutter button (large, 80px circle, primary color).
- Bottom-left flip-camera icon (in case Rashid is using the front camera).
- A small text label above the viewfinder: "একই কোণ থেকে ছবি তুলুন / Take the photo from the same angle as the before."
- A small photo counter at the top-right: "১ / ৩ / 1 of 3" (default: 1 of 3 for most categories; 1 of 2 for narrow categories).

### Capture flow

- Tap shutter → capture animation; viewfinder freezes for 500ms.
- Captured image is added to a horizontal thumbnail strip below the viewfinder.
- After the first photo is captured, the shutter becomes "পরবর্তী ছবি / Next photo" until the cap is reached.
- Three actions appear below the thumbnail strip:
  - "আবার তুলুন / Retake" (text link — retakes the most recent shot)
  - "গ্যালারি থেকে বাছাই / Choose from gallery" (text link)
  - "ছবি মুছুন / Remove" (only on photos beyond the first)

### Photo cap by category

- **Default cap: 3 photos.** Sufficient for road damage, waterlogging, and most multi-angle scenarios.
- **Narrow categories cap: 2 photos.** Configured per category in admin (e.g., single streetlight, single sign) where a wide shot isn't needed.
- **Mandatory minimum: 1 photo.** Resolution cannot be submitted without at least one "after" photo.

### Form section (inside overlay sheet at all viewports)

- **`field-job-resolve-form`** — collapsed by default, expands once a photo is captured. Renders inside the overlay sheet at every viewport.

#### Resolution note (optional)

- **`field-job-resolve-input-note`** — labeled textarea, 200 chars.
- **Label (BN):** "কী করা হয়েছে? (ঐচ্ছিক)"
- **Label (EN):** "What was done? (optional)"
- **Placeholder (BN):** "যেমন: ফিউজ পরিবর্তন, তার সংযোগ পুনঃস্থাপন"
- **Placeholder (EN):** "e.g., replaced fuse, reconnected wiring"

#### Parts used (optional)

- **`field-job-resolve-input-parts`** — labeled text input, 80 chars.
- **Label (BN):** "ব্যবহৃত যন্ত্র/উপকরণ"
- **Label (EN):** "Parts used"
- **Placeholder:** "যেমন: ৫A ফিউজ, ২ মিটার তার"

### Primary action (in-flow, inside overlay sheet)

- **`field-job-resolve-button-submit`** — primary CTA, in-flow at the bottom of the overlay sheet (after the note and parts inputs), full-overlay-width. **Not** sticky.
  - **Label (BN):** "সমাধান জমা দিন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Submit resolution"
  - Disabled until at least one photo is captured (cap reached OR user taps "Done with photos").
  - Tap → POSTs `{ticket_id, after_photos: [...], note?, parts?}` to `/api/v1/field/jobs/{id}/resolve`.

## Behavior

### Camera fallback

- If camera access is denied or unavailable: show gallery picker as the primary option, with text "ক্যামেরা অনুপলব্ধ — গ্যালারি থেকে নির্বাচন করুন / Camera unavailable — choose from gallery."

### Compression

- Each photo is compressed to 85% JPEG before upload (matches citizen-side report flow). Resolution capped at 1920×1080.
- Multi-photo uploads are parallelized; total payload for 3 photos is < 1.5 MB after compression.

### Offline upload

- If offline at submit time: photos are queued locally with the metadata. Submission appears successful in the UI; a small banner says "অফলাইনে জমা হয়েছে — সংযোগ ফিরে আসলে আপলোড হবে / Saved offline — will upload when reconnected."

### Image preview

- Captured thumbnail is tappable to expand to a larger preview with retake/keep options.

### Helper overlay (?)

- Shows two example photos: one "good" after photo (well-lit, same angle, clearly shows the fix), one "bad" after photo (blurry, wrong angle, or doesn't show the fix). Text caption: "এমন ছবি তুলুন যা সমস্যার সমাধান প্রমাণ করে / Take a photo that proves the fix."

## States

### Default (no capture)

Camera viewfinder live; counter shows "০ / ৩ / 0 of 3"; Submit disabled.

### One captured

Thumbnail strip with 1 photo; counter shows "১ / ৩ / 1 of 3"; Submit enabled.

### Cap reached (3 photos)

Shutter becomes disabled (greyed); counter shows "৩ / ৩ / 3 of 3"; "Done with photos" text link appears above Submit.

### Submitting

Spinner on Submit; form disabled; viewfinder hidden.

### Submitted

Routes to Confirmation page (see spec); success.

### Camera denied

Gallery picker is the only path; form section visible immediately.

### Offline submit

Success animation; offline banner appears; submission queued (all photos held locally).

### Photo rejected (server)

Server returns 422 if any photo fails quality heuristics (e.g., too blurry, wrong content). Modal: "ছবি প্রমাণ হিসেবে যথেষ্ট স্পষ্ট নয় — আবার তুলুন / The photo isn't clear enough — retake." Identifies which photo by index.

### Network failure

Toast; form re-enabled.

---

## Success

Rashid frames the shot, taps shutter, adds a quick note ("replaced fuse"), taps Submit. Confirmation page appears. Total time: under 30 seconds.

**Emotional outcome:** Rashid feels the platform **closes the loop for him, too**. He took the proof; the system does the rest.

---

## Open Questions

- **Quality heuristics** — server-side blur / content checks are best-effort. **Default for v1:** server rejects only on hard failures (no photo, corrupt file). Visual-quality heuristics are a v1.1 enhancement.
- **Multiple "after" photos** — **v1 default (revised after UX review):** 2–3 photos per resolution. Default cap = 3; narrow categories (configured per-category in admin) = 2. Mandatory minimum = 1. Multi-photo is a resolution-evidence requirement, not a v1.1 enhancement.
