# Confirmation — Mark Resolved

**Route:** `/field/jobs/{ticket_id}/resolved`
**Scenario:** Rashid the Resolver — Fixes his first job of the day (screen 5 of 5)
**Purpose:** Acknowledge Rashid's work, show what's next, and keep his momentum going.

> **Phase B (2026-10-04):** Confirmation is a **centered card on a neutral surface** (max-width 640px) at every viewport, mirroring the Submitted Confirmation pattern in 1.11. No top bar, no chrome — the centered card is the page. The card carries hero check + headline + photo pair recap + quick stats + two-button CTA pair (next job / back home). See `_progress/00-design-log.md` for the re-skin.

---

## User Context

Rashid has just finished his first job. He's tired, on a ladder, sweaty. He taps Submit, and the app responds. He wants to see "yes, that worked," "what's next," and feel like the day is moving.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `field-job-resolved-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Web-first **centered card** on `--color-surface-page`, max-width 640px (uses `--card-max-width` token), centered horizontally and vertically with `--space-7` top/bottom padding. The card uses `--color-surface-card` with `--radius-lg` corners, `--shadow-md` shadow.
- **At every viewport** (phone 360px → desktop 1440px), the card is identical in dimensions, padding, and content. There is no responsive reflow.
- **No top bar / no header** on this page. The centered card is the only content.

### Header

- No header. The card is the page.

### Hero block (inside the card)

- **`field-job-resolved-hero`** — centered, generous vertical padding.
- **Confirmation icon** — a 72px circular check-mark icon, `--color-status-resolved-soft` background, `--color-status-resolved` stroke. Centered.
- **`field-job-resolved-title`** — H1, centered, "কাজ সম্পন্ন / Job complete."
- **`field-job-resolved-subtitle`** — body, centered, `--color-ink-secondary`, "প্রমাণ ডিসপ্যাচারের কাছে পাঠানো হয়েছে — নিশ্চিত হলে নোটিফিকেশন যাবে / Proof sent to the dispatcher — they'll notify the citizen once confirmed."

### Photo pair recap (inside the card)

- **`field-job-resolved-photo-pair`** — two-column grid (1fr / 1fr) inside the card. ~140×140 each, with "আগে / Before" label on the left (single citizen photo) and "পরে / After" label on the right (one or more crew photos — show a "+২ / +2" overlay if there are 2–3 photos, per UX Review revision #6).
- Tap → opens lightbox with all photos in a horizontal scroll.

### Quick stats strip (inside the card, three columns)

- **`field-job-resolved-stats`** — three small stat tiles in a 1fr / 1fr / 1fr grid.
- **Tiles:**
  - "আজকের সম্পন্ন / Done today" — count (incremented)
  - "এই সপ্তাহে / This week" — count
  - "গড় সময় / Avg time" — duration

### Primary action (in-flow, inside the card, two-button grid)

- **`field-job-resolved-button-next-job`** — primary CTA, left half of the 1fr / 1fr grid. 64px tall.
  - **Label (BN):** "পরবর্তী কাজে যান"
  - **Label (EN):** "Move to next job"
  - Tap → routes to the next job's assignment detail page. If no next job: routes to `/field` (home).

### Secondary actions (inside the card)

- **`field-job-resolved-button-home`** — secondary outline, right half of the CTA grid.
  - **Label (BN):** "হোমে ফিরে যান"
  - **Label (EN):** "Back to home"
  - Tap → routes to `/field`.
- **`field-job-resolved-link-share-proof`** — text link below the CTA pair, centered.
  - **Label (BN):** "প্রমাণ শেয়ার করুন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Share proof"
  - Tap → opens OS share sheet with the photo + ticket ID + a one-line caption "CityFix-এ সমাধান: #{id}."

## Behavior

### Confirmation animation

- The check icon animates in with a subtle 200ms scale-and-fade. **No confetti.** Restrained celebration — earned emotion, not performative.

### Stat increment

- "Done today" and "This week" tiles update immediately from local cache; re-sync with server when network returns.

### Auto-route

- If Rashid has another job and does not interact with this page within 5 seconds, the page auto-routes to the next job. **Default for v1: no** (let Rashid breathe); the auto-route is a v1.1 "rapid mode" setting.

### Photo zoom

- Tap a thumbnail → opens lightbox with pinch-to-zoom.

### Offline submission

- If the resolve was queued offline, this page still renders; a small banner says "অফলাইনে জমা — সংযোগ ফিরে আসলে ডিসপ্যাচার দেখবেন / Saved offline — dispatcher will see when reconnected."

### Confirmation persistence

- This page does not poll. Once rendered, it stays static. The dispatcher confirmation flow (Resolve Confirmation modal in Habib's surface) is independent.

## States

### Default (just submitted, online)

Hero check + headline + subtitle + photo pair + quick stats + two-button CTA pair + share link, all inside the centered 640px card. No chrome.

### Offline submission

Offline banner above the hero block, inside the card (replaces the empty space above the photo pair).

### Photo not yet uploaded

Hero text changes slightly: "ছবি সংযোগ ফিরে আসলে আপলোড হবে / Photo will upload when reconnected."

### Loading stats

Stat tiles show placeholder dashes during initial render.

### Network failure (rare on this page)

Toast at the bottom of the page; stats stay cached.

## Success

Rashid sees the confirmation, sees his daily count tick up, taps Next job. He's 30 seconds into his next assignment with momentum intact.

**Emotional outcome:** Rashid feels his work **counts**. The platform gives him a quiet high-five and keeps him moving. He's not waiting for a "thank you" email — the platform's pace is his thanks.

---

## Open Questions

- **Gamification** — should Rashid get badges for streaks or volume? **Default for v1: no.** Internal-city-corp compensation and recognition is a city-policy matter, not a product feature. Light stats are sufficient.
- **Day-end summary** — at end-of-shift, a one-screen summary of his day. **Default for v1: no.** Stats on Home page are sufficient; the end-of-day screen is a v1.1 enhancement.
