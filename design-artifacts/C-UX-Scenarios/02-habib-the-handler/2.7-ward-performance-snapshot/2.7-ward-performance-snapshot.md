# Ward Performance Snapshot

**Route:** `/dispatch/insights` or `/dispatch/tickets/{ticket_id}` → "Insights" tab
**Scenario:** Habib the Handler — Runs his ward's morning queue (screen 7 of 7)
**Purpose:** Give Habib a high-level view of how his ward is performing — so he can report up the chain and adjust his own priorities.

---

## User Context

Habib's supervisor has asked for a weekly status. Habib wants a page he can pull up on a projector in a meeting — calm, factual, and free of vanity metrics. This is the page he screenshots.

---

## Content & Actions

### Page container

- **Page name:** `dispatch-insights-page`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Single-column report layout. Cards stacked vertically. Print-friendly.
- **Width:** capped at 960px.

### Header

- **`dispatch-insights-header`** — height 64px, sticky.
- **Left:** back arrow `<`. Routes to `/dispatch`.
- **Center:** "ওয়ার্ড ১৪ · কর্মক্ষমতা প্রতিবেদন / Ward 14 · Performance snapshot."
- **Right:** date range picker (default: last 7 days).

### Hero stats (top of body)

- **`dispatch-insights-hero-stats`** — three large stat cards in a row.
- **Cards:**
  - "মোট রিপোর্ট গৃহীত / Total reports received" — count + delta vs. previous period.
  - "সমাধানের হার / Resolution rate" — percentage + delta.
  - "গড় সমাধানের সময় / Avg time to resolve" — duration + delta.

### Charts section

- **`dispatch-insights-charts`** — three stacked charts.

#### Submitted vs. resolved over time

- Line chart. X-axis: dates (last 7 days). Y-axis: count. Two lines (submitted, resolved). Subtle grid. Tooltip on hover shows date + counts.

#### Category breakdown

- Horizontal bar chart. Categories on the y-axis, count on x-axis. Sorted by frequency.

#### Status breakdown (current snapshot)

- Donut chart. Submitted / Routed / In progress / Resolved segments with counts. Click a segment → drill-down to a filtered ticket list (modal).

### Crew performance table

- **`dispatch-insights-crew-table`** — sortable table.
- **Columns:**
  - Crew name
  - Specialty
  - Jobs completed (this period)
  - Avg time to resolve
  - Citizen feedback (positive / negative ratio)
- Sortable by any column.
- Highlight crews with low feedback ratio (red badge).

### Long-tail unresolved list

- **`dispatch-insights-long-tail`** — list of the 10 oldest still-open tickets in this ward.
- Each row: ticket ID + category + submitted timestamp + age + currently-assigned crew.
- Tap → opens ticket detail.

### Export controls

- **`dispatch-insights-button-export-pdf`** — primary button at the bottom.
  - **Label (BN):** "PDF হিসেবে রপ্তানি করুন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Export as PDF"
- Tap → generates a printable PDF with the same layout.
- **`dispatch-insights-button-export-csv`** — secondary text link.
  - **Label (BN):** "CSV ডাউনলোড"
  - **Label (EN):** "Download CSV"

---

## Behavior

### Date range selection

- Default: last 7 days. Options: today, last 7 days, last 30 days, custom.
- Selecting a range refreshes all charts and stats.

### Real-time vs. snapshot

- This page is a **snapshot** — it does not poll. It is refreshed manually or when the date range changes.

### Drill-down

- Clicking a chart segment opens a modal with a filtered list of tickets matching that segment. The list is the same component as the dashboard's ticket list.

### Print stylesheet

- The page has a print stylesheet that hides the back arrow, date picker, and export controls. Charts render in print-friendly mode (no shadows, larger fonts).

### Accessibility

- All charts have an accompanying table that screen-readers can navigate. Helper: `<table>` siblings to each chart with the same data.

---

## States

### Default

Last 7 days; all charts populated; hero stats populated.

### Empty (no data in range)

Hero stats show "০ / 0"; charts show empty-state messages: "এই সময়ে কোনো রিপোর্ট নেই / No reports in this period."

### Long-tail empty

"এই মুহূর্তে কোনো পুরোনো খোলা রিপোর্ট নেই / No long-tail unresolved reports right now." (A welcome sign — celebrate the clean queue.)

### Loading

Skeleton charts + stat cards.

### Exporting

Spinner on the export button; modal shows "PDF তৈরি হচ্ছে / Preparing your PDF…"

---

## Success

Habib pulls up the snapshot in his supervisor's office. They look at the resolution rate (87%, up from 79% last week), the category breakdown (streetlights still dominate), and the long-tail (3 tickets over 7 days, all with legitimate delays). He exports a PDF and emails it. The supervisor thanks him.

**Emotional outcome:** Habib feels the platform **backs his story with data**. He is not making excuses — the numbers are visible, the trends are real, and the long-tail is small.

## Open Questions

- **Citizen feedback ratio** — comes from Rahim's "+/-" prompt on resolved tickets. **Default for v1:** show the ratio (positive / negative) only when ≥ 5 tickets have feedback; otherwise show "পর্যাপ্ত তথ্য নেই / Not enough data."
- **Comparison vs. other wards** — Habib sees only his ward by default. A toggle "তুলনা / Compare" would show his ward vs. the city average. **Default for v1: yes**, available as a chart option.
