# Assign to Crew (modal)

**Route:** `/dispatch/tickets/{ticket_id}` → opens modal
**Scenario:** Habib the Handler — Runs his ward's morning queue (screen 4 of 7)
**Purpose:** Let Habib pick the right crew for a ticket in 3–5 seconds, with enough context to make a confident choice.

---

## User Context

Habib has just decided that this ticket needs a field crew. He needs to pick which one — based on availability, current load, specialty, and proximity. This modal is **his bridge** between desk work and the field.

---

## Content & Actions

### Modal container

- **Page name:** `dispatch-assign-crew-modal`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Modal overlay on top of the ticket detail page. Centered, max-width 640px on desktop, full-screen sheet on mobile.
- **Backdrop:** dim the underlying ticket detail by 40%.

### Header

- **`assign-crew-modal-header`** — H2. "ক্রু নির্বাচন করুন" / "Choose a crew."
- Subtitle: "কোন ক্রু এই রিপোর্টটি পাবে?" / "Which crew should take this?"

### Crew list

- **`assign-crew-modal-list`** — vertical list of crew cards.
- **`assign-crew-modal-crew-card-{crew_id}`** — each card shows:
  - **Crew name** — e.g., "ক্রু বি / Crew B"
  - **Specialty** — e.g., "বৈদ্যুতিক / Electrical" (matches the ticket's department)
  - **Current load** — "৩টি কাজ চলমান / 3 jobs in progress" (red badge if > 5)
  - **Today so far** — "২টি সমাধান / 2 resolved today"
  - **Last activity** — "৮ মিনিট আগে / 8 min ago"
  - **Distance to ticket** — "০.৮ কিমি দূরে / 0.8 km away" (computed from crew's last known location if shared)
- Sorted by best fit first: specialty match → current load (low first) → distance (near first) → last activity (recent first).
- A small "best match" tag on the top card.

### Empty state (no crews available)

- If no crews match the specialty: a friendly explanation: "এই বিভাগে কোনো ক্রু নেই — নতুন ক্রু যোগ করতে প্রশাসকের সাথে যোগাযোগ করুন / No crews for this department — contact the administrator to add a crew."
- A "ক্রু যোগ করুন / Add a crew" link (Habib cannot do this himself — opens an admin-mailer modal).

### Confirm action

- **`assign-crew-modal-button-confirm`** — primary button, sticky-bottom in the modal.
  - Disabled until a crew card is selected.
  - **Label (BN):** "বরাদ্দ করুন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Assign"
  - Tap → POSTs `{ticket_id, crew_id}` to `/api/v1/dispatch/assignments`.

### Cancel action

- **`assign-crew-modal-button-cancel`** — secondary text link.
  - **Label (BN):** "বাতিল"
  - **Label (EN):** "Cancel"
  - Tap → closes modal; ticket detail returns.

### Internal note composer (optional, collapsed by default)

- **`assign-crew-modal-note-composer`** — collapsible section.
- **Label (BN):** "ক্রুকে নির্দেশনা দিন (ঐচ্ছিক)"
- **Label (EN):** "Instructions for the crew (optional)"
- A 200-char textarea. Submitting with a note attaches it to the assignment and surfaces it in Rashid's assignment detail.

---

## Behavior

### Selection state

- Clicking a card highlights it and selects it.
- Clicking the highlighted card again deselects it.

### Submit flow

1. Tap Assign → button shows spinner; modal content disabled.
2. App POSTs `{ticket_id, crew_id, note?}` to `/api/v1/dispatch/assignments`.
3. **On 200 OK:** modal closes; ticket detail's status trail updates to show "In progress" with the assigned crew; success toast "ক্রু বি-তে বরাদ্দ করা হয়েছে / Assigned to Crew B."
4. **On 409 conflict** (crew just took another job): inline error "এই ক্রু এখন অন্য কাজে ব্যস্ত — অন্য ক্রু নির্বাচন করুন / This crew just took another job — pick a different crew."
5. **On 5xx / network failure:** toast "সংযোগ ব্যর্থ — আবার চেষ্টা করুন / Connection failed — try again."

### Auto-refresh of crew list

- The list polls every 15s while the modal is open, so Habib sees fresh load numbers.

### Keyboard shortcuts

- `↑` / `↓` — navigate crew cards.
- `Enter` — confirm selection.
- `Esc` — close modal.

---

## States

### Default (no selection)

All cards visible; Confirm disabled.

### Crew selected

Selected card highlighted; Confirm enabled.

### Submitting

Spinner on Confirm; cards disabled.

### Crew conflict (409)

Inline error at the top of the modal; conflicting crew card marked with a small warning icon; Confirm re-enabled with the new selection.

### Network failure

Toast at bottom of modal; cards re-enabled.

### Empty (no crews for this department)

Centered explanation + "Add a crew" link.

---

## Success

Habib selects "Crew B — Electrical" from the top of the list (best match), adds a one-line note about the specific streetlight pole number, taps Assign. The ticket detail updates; Rashid sees the new assignment on his phone within seconds.

**Emotional outcome:** Habib feels the dispatch loop is **fast and trustworthy** — the platform helps him pick the right crew, not just any crew.

---

## Open Questions

- **Crew location sharing** — distance-to-ticket requires the crew's last known location. **Default for v1:** approximate, based on Rashid's last seen ticket location (not GPS). True GPS is a v1.1 enhancement with explicit consent.
- **Bulk assignment** — Habib may want to assign 5 tickets at once from the dashboard. **Default for v1:** single-assign modal. Bulk-assign UI is a v1.1 follow-up.
