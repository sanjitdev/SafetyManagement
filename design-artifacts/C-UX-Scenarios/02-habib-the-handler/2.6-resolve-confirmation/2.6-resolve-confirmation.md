# Resolve Confirmation (modal)

**Route:** `/dispatch/tickets/{ticket_id}` → opens modal
**Scenario:** Habib the Handler — Runs his ward's morning queue (screen 6 of 7)
**Purpose:** Let Habib confirm a ticket as resolved — with the proof — and notify every linked citizen.

---

## User Context

Habib is reviewing a ticket where Rashid has uploaded an "after" photo. The "before / after" pair tells the story. Habib wants to mark this resolved, push the proof to every reporter linked to the ticket, and move to the next one.

---

## Content & Actions

### Modal container

- **Page name:** `dispatch-resolve-confirm-modal`
- **Language:** Bangla default.
- **Layout:** Modal overlay on the ticket detail page. Centered, max-width 720px on desktop (wider than other modals to accommodate the photo pair), full-screen on mobile.
- **Backdrop:** dim the underlying page by 40%.

### Header

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-header`** — H2. "সমাধান নিশ্চিত করুন / Confirm resolution."
- Subtitle: "এই রিপোর্টটি সমাধান হিসেবে চিহ্নিত করতে চান?" / "Mark this report as resolved?"

### Before / After preview

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-photo-pair`** — photos arranged based on count.
  - **Single after-photo:** side-by-side, 240×180 each on desktop.
  - **2–3 after-photos:** "Before" on the left (single image), all "After" photos in a horizontal scroll on the right with a "+n" indicator on overflow.
- "আগে / Before" label on the citizen's photo.
- "পরে / After" label on the first crew photo.
- Tap any photo → opens lightbox.

### Metadata recap

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-meta`** — small list.
- Category, ward, submitted timestamp, resolved timestamp.
- "ক্রু: ক্রু বি · ২ দিন ৪ ঘণ্টায় সমাধান / Crew: Crew B · resolved in 2 days 4 hours."

### Notification preview

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-notification-preview`** — small mock-up of the push notification reporters will receive.
  - Bangla: "আপনার #{id} রিপোর্ট সমাধান হয়েছে — দেখুন"
  - English: "Your #{id} report is resolved — view it"
- A small caption: "এই নোটিফিকেশন {n} জন রিপোর্টারকে পাঠানো হবে / This notification will be sent to {n} reporters."

### Confirm action

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-button-confirm`** — primary button, sticky-bottom.
  - **Label (BN):** "হ্যাঁ, সমাধান নিশ্চিত করুন"
  - **Label (EN):** "Yes, confirm resolution"
  - Tap → POSTs `{ticket_id, confirmed: true}` to `/api/v1/dispatch/resolve`.

### Cancel action

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-button-cancel`** — secondary text link.
  - **Label (BN):** "বাতিল"
  - **Label (EN):** "Cancel"
  - Tap → closes modal.

### Reject (if proof is insufficient)

- **`resolve-confirm-modal-button-reject`** — text link, red.
  - **Label (BN):** "প্রমাণ যথেষ্ট নয়"
  - **Label (EN):** "Proof is insufficient"
  - Tap → opens a sub-modal where Habib writes a short reason; submission rejects the resolution and sends Rashid back to in-progress with a note.

---

## Behavior

### Confirm flow

1. Tap Confirm → button shows spinner; modal disabled.
2. App POSTs `{ticket_id}` to `/api/v1/dispatch/resolve`.
3. **On 200 OK:** modal closes; ticket detail's status trail shows the resolution step complete; the push notification is dispatched to all linked reporters within 30 seconds (handled by the server); success toast: "সমাধান নিশ্চিত — {n} জন রিপোর্টারকে জানানো হয়েছে / Resolution confirmed — {n} reporters notified."
4. **On 5xx / network failure:** toast + retry.

### Reject flow

- Opens a textarea modal: "কেন প্রমাণ যথেষ্ট নয়? / Why is the proof insufficient?" with a 200-char limit.
- Submit → ticket reverts to in-progress, Rashid gets a new note in his assignment detail.

### Auto-refresh

- Modal polls every 15s to ensure the "after" photo and crew metadata are fresh.

### Keyboard shortcuts

- `Enter` — confirm.
- `Esc` — cancel.

---

## States

### Default

Photo pair visible; Confirm enabled; notification preview visible.

### Confirming

Spinner on Confirm.

### Confirmed

Modal closes; toast appears; ticket detail updates.

### Reject-mode

Modal swaps to rejection composer with reason field.

### Insufficient proof (reject submitted)

Modal closes; ticket detail shows a "প্রমাণ অপর্যাপ্ত — ব্যাখ্যা: … / Proof insufficient — reason: …" badge in the status trail.

### Network failure

Toast; modal re-enabled.

## Success

Habib sees the side-by-side photos, confirms the fix, the system notifies 4 reporters at once, the ticket moves off his queue, and the dashboard updates. He is back to scanning the next batch in under 60 seconds.

**Emotional outcome:** This is the **closing act** of the dispatcher's day — the moment when the chain he built (citizen → queue → department → crew → proof) comes together. Habib feels his work was worth it because the proof is right there.

## Open Questions

- **Re-notification of previously-notified reporters** — if Habib marks resolved, but then the citizen rejects the resolution and the crew re-attempts and re-marks resolved — does the citizen get a second push? **Default for v1: yes**, the second resolution is its own notification.
- **Resolve-on-behalf-of-crew** — sometimes Rashid marks in-progress but the ticket lingers; Habib may want to confirm-without-crew as an override. **Default for v1: no**, the modal requires an "after" photo. Override is a v1.1 special-tool.
