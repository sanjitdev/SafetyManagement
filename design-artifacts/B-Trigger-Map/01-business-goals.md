## Vision

CityFix is known as the way Dhaka gets things fixed. Citizens tell each other about it. Someone reports a problem that's been ignored for months or years, and within days or weeks it's resolved — and the citizen who reported it feels heard. The platform earns its reputation one fix at a time, and the city becomes a place where being heard actually moves something.

---

## Strategic Goals

### Goal 1 — Get things fixed in Dhaka

Citizens submit problems. Problems move through the queue. Things get fixed and the city gets visibly maintained. Citizens tell each other it works.

| # | Objective | Target |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1.1 | Reported problems reach a closed status at a rate most citizens experience as "it worked." | Specific closure-rate threshold set post-pilot with the operating authority (DNCC / DSCC). |
| 1.2 | Median time-to-resolution moves faster than today's "no feedback" baseline by a measurable margin. | Per-category SLAs (streetlight vs. road) calibrated post-pilot. |
| 1.3 | A meaningful share of citizens submit more than one report within 12 months — proof the first report earned a follow-through. | Specific share target set post-pilot based on Dhaka data. |

---

### Goal 2 — Make the city hear citizens

Authority staff respond visibly to citizen reports. Silence becomes hard to maintain. Citizens feel their complaint landed somewhere.

| # | Objective | Target |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 2.1 | Every submitted report produces a status trail the citizen can revisit (submitted → routed → in-progress → resolved). | 100% of reports. Hard, achievable from v1. |
| 2.2 | Overdue / aging tickets are surfaced publicly (e.g., ward-level dashboards) so silence is visible. | Live from v1; targets for "overdue" thresholds set per-category post-pilot. |
| 2.3 | Citizen satisfaction with the reporting experience itself (the "I was heard" feeling, separate from whether the fix happened) reaches a defined target. | Measured via in-app feedback after ticket close; target set post-pilot. |

---

### Goal 3 — Operate leanly across cities

Dhaka works. Adding Chattogram, Sylhet, and beyond doesn't require rebuilding. Each city's authority configures and runs independently.

| # | Objective | Target |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 3.1 | Onboarding a new city requires configuration only, not rebuild. | Per-city setup (categories, departments, wards, Bangla/English toggles) achievable within a target time window (set post-Dhaka-pilot). |
| 3.2 | Operating cost per citizen served stays within a defined ceiling. | Ceiling set with the operating authority post-pilot. |
| 3.3 | At least one additional major Bangladeshi city (Chattogram or Sylhet) is live on CityFix within 18 months of Dhaka launch. | Second city live within 18 months. |
