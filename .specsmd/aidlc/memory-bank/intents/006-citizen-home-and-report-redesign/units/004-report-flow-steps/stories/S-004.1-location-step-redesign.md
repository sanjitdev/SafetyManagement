# Story S-004.1 — Location step redesign (FR-4)

## As a

Citizen starting a new report

## I want

The location step to show a clear "Confirm location" header, a
short instruction, the reverse-geocoded address, the ward, the
coordinates in mono font, and a big "Continue" button

## So that

I can confirm the pin in 2 taps (one to adjust, one to continue)
without parsing a wall of map metadata.

## Acceptance Criteria (BDD)

### Given
The current `/report/location` page shows the map and a side card
with a list of metadata rows.

### When
The construction agent restructures the side card to:
- Header: "অবস্থান নিশ্চিত করুন" ("Confirm location")
- One-paragraph instruction (≤ 24 words, Bangla)
- Reverse-geocoded address (one line, secondary text)
- Ward name (one line, secondary text)
- Coordinates in mono font (caption)
- Big "Continue" primary button (full-width on mobile)
- Small "Pick on map" text button below
- The geofence-reject path renders as an inline error card with
  `--color-destructive` left border, X icon, one-sentence
  explanation, and "Adjust the pin" action.

### Then
- The card never exceeds 480px wide on desktop.
- On mobile (≤ 640px), the card is at the bottom 40% of the
  screen; the map is the top 60%.
- The geofence-reject card is rendered above the map at a 12dp
  margin.
- Tapping "Adjust the pin" calls `map.flyTo({ center, zoom: 16 })`
  and removes focus from the button.
- "Continue" is disabled until a pin is dropped (no default pin).
- axe-core shows zero serious or critical issues.

## Story Notes

- The reverse-geocoded address comes from the existing
  `/api/report/bootstrap` or a similar endpoint (no new endpoint).
- The geofence-reject path is per the v1 spec (R-1 in the design
  log); the new design surfaces the error inline instead of a
  modal.
