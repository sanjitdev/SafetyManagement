CityFix — Problem Description

In large cities, people regularly encounter broken streetlights, potholes, open drains, overflowing garbage, damaged roads, waterlogging, and other public infrastructure problems. However, there is often no simple, centralized way to report these issues, track their progress, or know whether they have actually been resolved.

Reports are scattered across phone calls, social media, messaging apps, and different government channels. This makes it difficult for citizens to know where to report a problem, who is responsible, and when it will be fixed.

CityFix would provide a single platform where citizens can report city problems with photos and locations, while authorities can verify, prioritize, assign, and track them through to resolution.

CityFix — Idea

A citizen-powered city issue reporting and tracking platform where people can report problems like potholes, broken streetlights, garbage, waterlogging, and open drains with photos and location. The system routes reports to the responsible authority, detects duplicate complaints, tracks resolution status, and lets citizens see whether and when the issue was fixed.
